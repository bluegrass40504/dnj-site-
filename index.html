About DNJ

Founded in Lexington by Juan Valladares.

DNJ Electrical Solutions is Juan's company. Juan started it in Lexington in June 2023, and it became an LLC in June 2024. We do commercial and industrial electrical work.

IEC Bluegrass is the group that runs electrical apprenticeship and licensing education in Kentucky. DNJ is a member.

Apprentice to foreman

Start on the tools. Grow into the lead.

On Juan's crew you learn the trade on real commercial jobs and take on more as you prove yourself.

  1. 01

    Apprentice

    You pull wire, bend conduit, and learn rough-in working next to an experienced electrician.

  2. 02

    Electrician

    You handle your own work on the job, from conduit runs to fixtures, and get it right the first time.

  3. 03

    Foreman

    You run the crew on site, keep the job safe and on track, and answer for the finished work.

What we do

Commercial and industrial electrical work.

From bare studs to a lit building, we handle the electrical.

  • 01

    Build-outs

    Full electrical for new commercial spaces and tenant build-outs, from rough-in to final.

  • 02

    Power and conduit

    Conduit runs, feeders, and power distribution laid out clean and to code.

  • 03

    Lighting and fixtures

    Interior and exterior lighting installed, wired, and tested before we leave.

  • 04

    Service upgrades and repairs

    Panel and service upgrades, troubleshooting, and repairs that keep your building running.
